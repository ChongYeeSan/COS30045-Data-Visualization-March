Ideas to Overcome

1) Opt for TVs with Energy Star labels designed to consume less power.
2) Optimise TV settings to reduce brightness and enable power saving mode.
3) Turn off the TV when not in use instead of leaving it on standby.

Our Goal

Reduce television energy usage by helping consumers identify the most energy-efficient screen technology — minimising electricity costs and carbon footprint.
